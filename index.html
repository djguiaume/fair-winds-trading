Fair Winds Trading

Sailing & Trading Prototype

  • The wind drives you. You are fastest with the wind across the boat and slower with it behind.
  • You can't sail into the wind. Point inside the red zone on the compass and the sails flap and the boat stops.
  • To go upwind, zigzag (tack): sail about 50° off the wind, then turn through it to the other side.
  • Trading & Ports: Slow down near any island dock to trade goods at the market, buy vessel upgrades, and purchase the cutter.
  • Open Map (M) to find ports and place markers. Only your chosen destination gets a bearing cue.
