**Fair Winds Trading Co. 1.3.1, "Boats that ride them".** This is the build the game menu shows as **v1.3.335**.
Play it at https://djguiaume.github.io/fair-winds-trading/ (desktop, tablet, phone and the Xbox browser, installable).

### Boats that ride them
- **Your ship rides the sea**: it rises, pitches and rolls with the swell, gently (bigger ships roll less), with a slight lean into the wind. It follows Settings › Gentle sea motion and the Waves switch, and stays still on Low and Ultra-low.
- **Waves slow the boat a little**: head seas take up to 10% off your speed, following seas almost nothing. A small "Waves −3%" line shows it on the compass card, and Settings › Waves slow the boat turns it off. Race pars were re-sailed; the sharp first-upgrade target is now 5.25 min.
- **The surf looks like surf**: thin breaking lines run in to the shore, and each swell washes a foam edge and a darker wet-sand band up the beach and back (fewer lines at 14 kn, more in a storm, half at night).
- **A faster first look**: the phone shows a start view straight away while the game loads.
- **Look touch-ups**: Rimewick's snow shelf is fixed and Amber Strand has rock pools.

### For the makers
- The builder's Terrain tab has **Bake coast**, and `npm run island -- <id> bake-coast` does the same through one shared module.
- Ride tuning is in `waves.json › motion.ride`; the speed rule is `src/sim/seaDrag.ts`.
