**Fair Winds Trading Co. 1.7, "Tidy, smooth, shiny".** This is the build the game menu shows as **v1.7.392**.
Play it at https://djguiaume.github.io/fair-winds-trading/ (desktop, tablet, phone in landscape and the Xbox browser, installable).

### Shinier on Ultra
- **Boat and island reflections**: on Ultra the sea mirrors the boats, the islands and the drawn sky. Settings › Display › "Boat and island reflections" switches it off. High has a cheaper variant, off until a device run.

### Life in the towns
- **People on the quays**: walkers in the streets, fishermen on the piers and dock workers by the cargo, more of them as a town grows. Switchable in Settings; fewer on Low, none on Ultra-low.

### Smoother on a phone
- **Phone fluidity targets**: seven measurable criteria (frame pacing, input response, loading, heat, effects, textures) now guide the phone `?bench` runs.

### For the makers
- The quest code is 26 files under 400 lines instead of five big ones, and the big editor and `?tune` stylesheets are split into parts. No behaviour change; the built CSS and a saved quest are the same.
- Open: real-device `?bench` for reflections and town folk (#377); `builder.ts` split (#492).
