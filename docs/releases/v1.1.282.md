**Fair Winds Trading Co. 1.1, "The Sailing Club".** This is the build the game menu shows as **v1.1.282**.
Play it at https://djguiaume.github.io/fair-winds-trading/ (desktop, tablet, phone and the Xbox browser, installable).

### The Sailing Club
- **Wren opens a club house at Abbey Head**: a pier with a racer café and her shed. Rook drops a hint after the prologue and marks Abbey Head on your chart.
- **Eight new races**: three round the home waters, three with a squall to sail round, the Meridian Run and the Great Reach. Bronze, silver and gold on each, on all three levels.
- **A club board** on the Abbey Head Quay: every race with your best time, your medal and the par for your ship, and a Race again button. Best times are kept per boat class.
- **Squalls sit in the same place every attempt**, so a course with one stays fair. With Storms switched off in Settings it is plain rain and a wind shift.
- **The Tern**: win the club pennant and Wren hands you a fast, light racing boat with a small hold. The Pilot and the Yawl are sold at Abbey Head afterwards.

### Turn the camera
- Right stick on a controller (click it to reset), right-drag on a PC, two fingers on a phone or tablet. The wind compass and the rain turn with it. Switch it off in Settings.

### Looks
- **Ultra quality** for desktop and the Xbox: soft shadows, ambient occlusion, a sky gradient, better night light and a lower camera tilt. Phones and the old tablet look and run as before.
- **The water reflects the sky**, with softer foam on the shores.
- **A new look for the menus**: the title menu, the port screen, the chart, Settings and the quest cards are now paper, dark wood and brass, with a serif for names, and a more readable wind and speed log. Same screens, same places.

### For the makers
- Races list their own storms (`npm run race -- <id> --storm`, and the quest builder), and checks and balance bots cover them.
- GitHub Actions now post screenshot sheets, short failing-line comments and rule checks on pull requests.
