**Fair Winds Trading Co. 1.13, "Soft and rich".** This is the build the game menu shows as **v1.13.466**.
Play it at https://djguiaume.github.io/fair-winds-trading/ (desktop, tablet, phone in landscape and the Xbox browser, installable).

### Soft and detailed
- **Texture near the camera**: soft surface patterns on boards, hulls and sails that fade with distance, smoother hulls, softer shadows on High.

### Up close
- **A helmsman at the tiller**, sheets, and **spray** that grows with speed; a small camera lean you can switch off.
- **Follow cam sound**: hull, sail and rigging closer, a creak on gusts, a rattle on tacks.

### Screens and fixes
- **Boat previews on the real sea and sky** in Boat looks and Free Sailing.
- Free Sailing setup: wider cards, even boat type chips.
- Phone: the view button no longer sits over the wind compass.
