**Phase 4 is complete: four new seas beyond the reef.** This is the build the game menu shows as **v0.4.226**.
Play it at https://djguiaume.github.io/fair-winds-trading/ (desktop, tablet, phone and the Xbox browser, installable).

### Four seas around the ring
- The Windward Ring is now the heart of a sea five times bigger, split into four quarters. Each new sea starts as blank parchment and is charted as you sail it.
- **South, Sunreach:** a ring-shaped lagoon port behind the Coral Line, trade winds, the doldrums of the Glass, and sand shallows only shallow keels can cross.
- **West, Emberhold:** black sand and basalt, lava rivers, ash clouds and a warm current. The Smoulder smokes and sometimes erupts, and one eruption raises a brand-new island.
- **North, Rimewick:** a snowy fjord town, drifting icebergs, cold gales and a cove locked in pack ice.
- **East, Fogwatch:** fog banks that close in, the Race, sea stacks and the Stormbreak, a belt of squalls with lulls you can time.
- Wind zones and currents change how you sail each sea. Every hazard has its own switch in Settings.

### Twelve new stories and four outposts
- A letter from a relative beyond the reef opens each sea. In each sea you help the keeper of an old craft (tea, glass, jam, amber), then bring a settler out to found an outpost: Palm Key, Cinder Isle, Snowberry Isle and Amber Strand.
- The seas open early, each one right after its ring story, and run side by side. A last beat with Rook at Skerry closes the voyage once all four outposts stand.
- Outposts gather raw goods, the keepers craft them, and the ring's towns pay well for them: spiced tea, obsidian mirrors, cloudberry jam and amber beads.

### Easier to start, easier to learn
- Gentler prologue, people who speak to you on the harbour's Quay tab, and a replayable **Sailing lessons** course with Rook from the menu.
- Run short of gold? You can take a load on credit, and the game warns you before you spend yourself short.

### Play with a controller
- Sail, read the chart and use every menu and the port screen with an Xbox or any standard controller, including in the Xbox's Edge browser (TV mode with safe margins). On-screen hints show the right buttons for how you are playing.

### For the makers
- The map editor now edits every sea file. The quest builder writes, checks and play-tests quests from any step, and a Controls page edits the controller mapping.
- Command-line tools give the same edits and checks to agents: `npm run check`, `balance`, `island`, `sea`, `chart`, `shots`, `quest`.
- Balance bots play the whole story in order on all three levels: a relaxed captain founds the last outpost after about 2 h 10 to 2 h 30.
