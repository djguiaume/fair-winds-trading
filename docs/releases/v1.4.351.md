**Fair Winds Trading Co. 1.4, "Visible upgrades".** This is the build the game menu shows as **v1.4.351**.
Play it at https://djguiaume.github.io/fair-winds-trading/ (desktop, tablet, phone and the Xbox browser, installable).

### Your own boat
- **Upgrades you can see**: better sails get a coloured foot band, then a head band and stripe; a stronger hull gets a tan, then brass rubbing strake and brighter copper. Every class, day and night.
- **Boat looks**: a new screen from the shipyard. Pick sail colours, six patterns, twelve logos, hull colours, a figurehead, a flag and a wake style, try them on a turning boat, compare stock and upgraded looks, then Use. Each ship keeps her own look.
- **Earned, never sold**: colours and patterns are free; logos, figureheads and wakes come from the story, the towns and the regattas. The locked ones say how to earn them.
- **Sail marks on the cloth**: logos and your racing number now follow the sail as it fills and furls.
- **Free Sailing**: a new title menu row. Pick a place, a time and the weather, any ship and upgrades, and just sail. Nothing is kept: your voyage, gold, quests and race records are never touched.

### Also
- Text across the game follows one type scale (sentence case, no tracking).
- Settings has a switch for wake styles.

### For the makers
- Cosmetics are one JSON file each (plus an SVG for logos) in `src/data/cosmetics/`, checked by `npm run check`; the editor's Cosmetics page and `npm run cosmetics` share one module.
- Free Sailing presets are data in `src/data/free-sailing/`, with `npm run free`.
- Quests can give a ship and start when a regatta series is won.
