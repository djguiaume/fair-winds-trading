**Phase 2 is complete: living settlements.** This is the build the game menu shows as **v0.2.73**.
Play it at https://djguiaume.github.io/fair-winds-trading/ (desktop, tablet and phone, installable).

### Towns that grow
- Every port has a level, from hamlet to port town: selling the goods it wants and delivering its orders makes it grow, with more buildings, a bigger dock and its own island shape.
- Each town opens a workshop at level 3 (smoked fish, bread, planks, cut stone) and the other ports start buying it.
- The far yards make rare goods once they grow: honey at Abbey Head, lamp oil at Watchman Key. A town needs its rare good to become a port town.
- A neglected town stalls and posts a help order with a better reward.

### Weather, day and night
- The wind wanders round the whole compass and differs across the sea.
- Calm, breeze, rain and gale periods, and storms that cross the map. Heel too hard and cargo goes overboard; the sails control warns you first.
- A 12-minute day with dusk, a moonlit night, lanterns and lit windows.
- Weather, storms and night can each be switched off in Settings.

### Choose your challenge
- Three difficulty levels, Fair winds, Captain and Old salt, change costs, pace and weather but never how the boat sails. Settings › Wind picks a steady or shifting wind.

### Ships and harbours
- Redesigned models for the hoy, fluyt and indiaman, with a shared square rig.
- Solid piers, quays and moored boats; docking follows the real hull shape.
- Shipyard top speeds in knots.

### Also new
- Move your save to another device with a QR code or a file.
- Sails stay as you left them when you resume at sea.
- A French translation pass with proper sailing words, and smoother play on older devices.
