**Fair Winds Trading Co. 1.10, "Villages".** This is the build the game menu shows as **v1.10.74**.
Play it at https://djguiaume.github.io/fair-winds-trading/ (desktop, tablet, phone in landscape and the Xbox browser, installable).

### Villages
- **Every town is an old European village now, not a grid**: lanes that curve round the shore, a small square or green at the heart, houses of mixed size at their own angles, gardens and fields beside them. Calda, Saltmere, Westholm and Stonehaven, Abbey Head and Watchman Key, then Rimewick, Snowberry Isle, Amber Strand, Fogwatch, Palm Key, Sunreach, Cinder Isle and Emberhold. Each keeps the feeling of its sea and trade. Drying racks are open frames and snow firs are lower, so the towns read right from the follow cam too.
- **A town check against grids** keeps it so for every future change.

### Night
- Every house shows a lit window from any side; the moon shows on Ultra again; lamps and the masthead light glow warm instead of white discs.

### Follow camera
- A soft wake with no chequer, thin wind streaks, lighthouse beams that look like light, and a softer sea mirror on Ultra.
- The port name on arrival sits low on the screen and no longer covers the town.

### Under the hood
- The classic look (`?look=v1`) and Kids mode are gone: less code, one render budget.
