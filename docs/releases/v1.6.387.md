**Fair Winds Trading Co. 1.6, "The far seas' story".** This is the build the game menu shows as **v1.6.387**.
Play it at https://djguiaume.github.io/fair-winds-trading/ (desktop, tablet, phone in landscape and the Xbox browser, installable).

### A story with a turn in every sea
- **Each far sea has its moment**: the doldrums in the south, the lull in the Stormbreak in the east, the ice breaking in the north, the ash wind in the west. Wren is there, and every one still finishes with its hazard switched off in Settings.
- **Outposts are one scene each**, and the settler's sibling from the ring comes along, so the reunions happen on screen (Ness, Ines, Kit and Maren).
- **A question to follow**: each sea washes up one odd thing carrying the same mark, a star over a wave. A short **chapter after Four Winds** answers it with Rook's father's mark, then brings the old flag and Ness home to Skerry for a feast on the pier.
- **Far goods travel**: each far keeper now also asks for another sea's good (Sigrun wants Juno's tea, Juno a mirror, Bram beads, Elias cloudberries), and settlers ask for timber, planks and bread instead of timber three times.
- **Smaller fixes**: Ness now sends you to Watchman Key (with a mark on the chart), and Wren's standing order is offered at Abbey Head, where she lives.

### A living sky and sea
- **A drawn sky** with a clear blue gradient, sun, moon, stars and clouds, and a brighter chase camera with a bluer sea.
- **Sea life near the boat**: gulls over harbours, terns, dolphins, an occasional whale and soft fish shoals. Settings › Sea life switches it off; Low keeps a few gulls, and Ultra-low shows none.

### For the makers
- Sea life is data (`src/data/sea-life.json`), edited on the editors' Sea life page and with `npm run life`. The new quests and asks use the quest engine and standing-order pools; lines are in `docs/story/v1.6/`.
- Open: real-device `?bench` runs for the sky and sea life (#377). People on the sea are planned separately (#477).
