**Phase 3 is complete: people, stories and your own island.** This is the build the game menu shows as **v0.3.129**.
Play it at https://djguiaume.github.io/fair-winds-trading/ (desktop, tablet and phone, installable).

### A new beginning: the foghorn
- The tutorial is now a short rescue at sea: alone in the fog, follow the foghorn, come alongside the wreck on East Reef without touching the reef and bring Harbourmaster Rook home to Saltmere.
- His thanks is your own island, Skerry.

### Four towns, four stories
- **Where the shoals went** (Saltmere, Kit): find the fish again, and a new gap opens through East Reef.
- **The last keel** (Port Calda): stone for the slipway, a mast fetched from Greyback Ridge where there is no harbour, and a pilot cutter launched.
- **The mill on the hill** (Westholm, Maren): timber, millstones from Stonehaven and warm bread rushed to Saltmere.
- **A light on the Teeth** (Stonehaven, Ada Flint): a lighthouse on Teeth Rock, with Old Ness and Rook.
- When all four are done, the epilogue **Fair winds** calls you home to Skerry, where your company's flag and name board go up.
- Quest cards and a journal, people drawn as low-poly portraits, each with a little babble voice (Settings › Voices).
- The towns change for good after each story: new buildings, lights, buoys and fishing boats, and new trade.

### Your home island
- Skerry has build spots. Each story lets you pick one of two buildings: workshop or rigging loft, warehouse or bakehouse, lookout or pilot house, weather glass or boathouse.
- The warehouse stores cargo while you are moored; the weather glass shows the wind a minute ahead under the compass.

### At sea and at night
- Lighthouses and beacons light the night, and island ramps, woods and lanes make every island its own.
- Boundary buoys show the edge of the sea; smoother play on older tablets and phones, ready for a much bigger map.
- Shorter save links (under 1000 characters) to move a voyage between devices.

### For the makers
- Almost everything is now data: goods, ships, town levels, weather, text, music, quests, people, buildings and props.
- Built-in tools: the map editor and island builder (`editor.html`) and a live tuning panel with a balance check (`?tune`).
