**Fair Winds Trading Co. 1.2, "A new look".** This is the build the game menu shows as **v1.2.313**.
Play it at https://djguiaume.github.io/fair-winds-trading/ (desktop, tablet, phone and the Xbox browser, installable).

### A new look
- **Smooth islands**: rolling ground, shores that slope into the sea, soft colour zones per sea, crisp cliffs and rocks where they should be. Stacks, volcano cones, ice and reefs keep their faceted shapes.
- **Blue water by depth**: turquoise shallows into deep blue, a light haze tinted per sea, deep-blue nights.
- **Forests**: pines, broadleaf, palms and scrub by sea, drawn efficiently. The east stays open heath.
- **Richer other boats**: fishing boats, coasters and visitors with new shapes, colours by home port, deck cargo and smaller wakes.
- **The old look is one switch away**: add `?look=v1` to the address.
- It holds 60 fps on a phone at Medium and on the Xbox at High.

### For the makers
- A shared **height field** drives the game, the editors and the checks. The island builder has **terrain brushes** (raise, lower, flatten, smooth, slope) with undo, saved as edits in the island file, and `npm run island -- <id> sculpt …` does the same from the command line.
- `npm run check` compares the drawn coast with the sailed coast; `npm run review` makes per-island High and Low sheets with a coast chart.
