**Fair Winds Trading Co. 1.12, "Light and water".** This is the build the game menu shows as **v1.12.456**.
Play it at https://djguiaume.github.io/fair-winds-trading/ (desktop, tablet, phone in landscape and the Xbox browser, installable).

### One world, two views
- **The same air from above and from behind**: sun, haze, sea colour and night match in both views, with the top view's vibrant look as the reference. `npm run shots -- --both` checks it.

### Light
- **Shadows you can see** from the follow cam, contact shade under things, a sun glitter path on the sea and warm sun through the sails.
- **Night has form**: a cool moon as key light, lamp pools on the ground, a moon path to the horizon.

### Water and sky
- **Waves you can see from behind the boat**, with chop in the lee and foam in fresh wind.
- **Life on the open sea from above**: cloud shadows and swell lines.
- **A richer sky**: two soft cloud layers, a sun disc and squalls you can see coming.

### Speed and fixes
- **Budgets in CI** for first load, the follow cam and every quality level; **Auto quality** scales the resolution first, then drops a level, and climbs back.
- Less sea life: not something next to you all the time.
