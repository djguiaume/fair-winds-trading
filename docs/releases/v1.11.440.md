**Fair Winds Trading Co. 1.11, "Welcome aboard".** This is the build the game menu shows as **v1.11.440**.
Play it at https://djguiaume.github.io/fair-winds-trading/ (desktop, tablet, phone in landscape and the Xbox browser, installable).

### The first minute
- **One click to sailing**: New voyage starts at once on Captain. The title keeps three rows, with a small "Level: Captain, change" line.
- **A short first voyage**: six hints (sails, wind, foghorn, wreck, aboard, home) with no "step 1 of 14". The trading hints come at the first market.
- **A better opening**: the first voyage starts on a morning sea with land ahead, seen from the follow cam.

### Follow camera
- **The same HUD in both views**, a view button on every input, and your chosen view is remembered (Settings › Display).
- **The boat sits low in the frame** so you see where you are going, and the camera button cycles through three framings and the top view.
- **Look round the boat** with the mouse, the stick or two fingers.

### Phone
- A compact landscape HUD: a sail lever on the right edge with the speed on it, Map, Cargo and Menu as icons, and a left-handed option.

### Fixes
- Low quality is smoother, with no square tiles in the water.
