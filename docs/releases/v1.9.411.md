**Fair Winds Trading Co. 1.9, "Believable towns".** This is the build the game menu shows as **v1.9.411**.
Play it at https://djguiaume.github.io/fair-winds-trading/ (desktop, tablet, phone in landscape and the Xbox browser, installable).

### Believable towns
- **Every town rebuilt to make sense**: houses on flat ground facing a lane, the quay or the water; streets and quays along the shore; fields and gardens beside the houses, woods on the slopes; the harbour at the heart of each town. All 16 towns, from Saltmere and Port Calda to Sunreach, Emberhold, Rimewick and Fogwatch. Every landmark and quest place stays where you know it.
- **Stonehaven** gets a harbour quarter under its cliff; **Westholm**'s church and bakery move down by the harbour.

### Smoother
- **No more stalls** once the sea is on screen: shaders are ready before the splash lifts, and island and town building is spread over several frames.

### Small fixes
- Lean-to roofs and chimney caps stand out from the main roof; a softer moon halo so windows and lanterns lead at night.

### Open
