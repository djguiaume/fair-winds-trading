**Phase 1 is complete: one-captain trading.** This is the build the game menu shows as **v0.1.50**.
Play it at https://djguiaume.github.io/fair-winds-trading/ (desktop, tablet and phone, installable).

### Sail
- Real wind sailing: tack upwind around the no-go zone, reach and run, with speed in knots and the point of sail on screen.
- The wind shifts every few minutes, and islands shape it: a calm lee, faster flanks and channels.
- Boats heel with the wind; reefs, shallows and crashes damage the hull, repaired in port.

### Trade
- The Windward Ring map: four trading ports around two barrier islands and the Narrows, plus two hidden shipwright ports to discover.
- Four goods with supply-and-demand prices, delivery orders that pay more for long hauls, and prices on demand on the chart.

### Grow your fleet
- Sloop and cutter upgrades (sails, hold, hull) shown on the boat, bought at your home port, Saltmere.
- Deliveries at Watchman Key and Abbey Head open the swift line (schooner, clipper) and the hauler line (hoy, fluyt, indiaman).

### Look and feel
- New navy-and-brass interface: sea HUD, full-screen port screen, clear full-screen chart, menus, settings and How to play.
- Optional first-voyage tutorial with coach marks, English and French, background music, auto-save and instant resume.

### Not in this release
Settlement levels and growing towns (PRs #42 and #43) are still open and start Phase 2.
