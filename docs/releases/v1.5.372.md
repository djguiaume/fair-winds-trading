**Fair Winds Trading Co. 1.5, "A real game".** This is the build the game menu shows as **v1.5.372**.
Play it at https://djguiaume.github.io/fair-winds-trading/ (desktop, tablet, phone in landscape and the Xbox browser, installable).

### A new look, everywhere
- **One identity, the chart table**: ink on paper with one sage accent, IM Fell English for titles and Alegreya Sans for the rest, torn paper edges. Every screen is built from the same few parts, so buttons, cards, rows, tabs and dialogs look and feel the same, and press, slide and fade the same way (and respect reduced motion).
- **Title, pause, settings and New voyage**: a logbook page beside the key art; a small pause card; four settings tabs; three New voyage cards. A paper loading screen with a boat on a wave line, and a page asking you to turn an upright phone sideways.
- **The sea HUD in clusters**: status top left, the heading strip, the wind compass with its hatched no-go zone top right, the speed as the biggest number, actions along the bottom. A quiet HUD setting and an idle fade; the sailing numbers stay visible by default.
- **The harbour as one ledger**: Market, Orders, Quay and Shipyard on one paper page under a banner of the port. **Every port has its own painted banner, day and night.** The scene is no longer shown through the paper.
- **The sea chart as a paper chart**: engraved coast, ink labels, a rose, and tabs for Chart, Journal, Cargo, Prices and Legend. All tabs, everywhere, are the same row in the same place.
- **Moments**: an arrival banner, coins that tick on a sale, a quest stamp, the race horn and finish board, nine new interface sounds and a title sting.
- **Quests and the rest**: the quest card never covers the quest line, the compass or the sail lever on a phone; Help, voyage transfer, the tutorial offer and coach marks, Free Sailing, the club board and every confirmation use the same parts. One step count in the quest chip and the tutorial.
- **A close chase camera**: an optional third-person view behind the boat (C key, a pad stick press or a pinch).

### For the makers
- The whole look is data: `src/data/ui/theme.json` (colours, type, spacing, sizes, motion), edited on the editors' Theme page or with `npm run theme`; `npm run tune` edits the tuning numbers, including the HUD's.
- The old look (dark teal and brass, Barlow, Fraunces) and its stylesheets are gone. `npm run shots` now takes desktop and phone landscape by default, and the pull request sheet follows.
- The painted port banners cost 364 CU in total, once.
