**Fair Winds Trading Co. 1.4.1, "Cosmetics and free sailing".** This is the build the game menu shows as **v1.4.360**.
Play it at https://djguiaume.github.io/fair-winds-trading/ (desktop, tablet, phone and the Xbox browser, installable).

### Free Sailing
- **Weather at a glance**: every Free Sailing preset card shows a small icon for its weather, so you see calm, breeze or storm before you pick.

### For the makers
- The icons are data (`weatherIcons` in the Free Sailing rules); `npm run free -- check` and the editor require one per weather.

The cosmetic catalogue, Boat looks and isolated Free Sailing were already part of v1.4.
