**Fair Winds Trading Co. 1.0: the base game is finished.** This is the build the game menu shows as **v1.0.253**.
Play it at https://djguiaume.github.io/fair-winds-trading/ (desktop, tablet, phone and the Xbox browser, installable).

From one sloop off Saltmere to four seas round the Windward Ring: read the wind, trade, help the islands' people and grow your own island, Skerry. A full voyage through the story takes about 2 to 4 hours, and the sea stays open after it.

### A living sea
- **Other boats sail with you.** Fishing boats work their grounds, coasters carry each town's goods to the towns that want them, and ships from the far seas call once their outposts stand. They sail by the same wind as you, tack upwind, keep clear of you and light a stern lantern at night.
- **The sea has sound.** Waves, wind and gulls change from sea to sea: kittiwakes and ice in the north, surf in the south, lava hissing by the vents in the west, foghorns in the east. Sails flap and slat when you luff in irons, hulls creak, harbours ring their bell.
- **Your hull shows its knocks.** Scuffs, then patched sails and a missing rail, then torn canvas and a list. A repair clears it all.

### The Saltmere Regatta
- **Wren Tally**, a friendly rival skipper, races her cutter *Kestrel* against you. Rook keeps the times in his book.
- Eight races: three round the ring after the epilogue, one in each sea after its outpost (the Coral Line shallows, a lap of the Smoulder, up the Floe Line, across the Stormbreak in a lull), and the **Four Winds Cup** to Skerry.
- Bronze, silver and gold on every course, a pennant at your masthead for each series won, and the Cup on Skerry's pier. Nothing fails, and every race can be sailed again.

### People keep talking
- Once their story is done, the islands' people ask again now and then: standing orders on the Quay tab, in their own voice, paid like a help order.
- Quests you have taken greet you at each stop with what changed, quest cargo rides on deck, and a timed run has its clock on screen. A Cargo tab on the chart shows the hold and the deck, and finished quests open in the Journal with everything that was said.

### Clearer and prettier
- **Skerry's build choice is clear:** the person who gives you a plot names the two buildings to choose from, the plot is staked out on your island, and Skerry's harbour screen has an Island tab.
- **Every harbour shows its port:** a picture of the town at its level and time of day, its keeper with a line, and how close it is to growing.
- **The Windward Ring caught up with the four seas:** banded cliffs, grown towns, Westholm's mill knoll, Saltmere's smokehouses, a wreck on the Teeth until the light is lit.
- The chart shows real course marks: the quest's place, and a mark of your own.
