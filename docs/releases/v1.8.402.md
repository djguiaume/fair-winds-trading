**Fair Winds Trading Co. 1.8, "A richer look".** This is the build the game menu shows as **v1.8.402**.
Play it at https://djguiaume.github.io/fair-winds-trading/ (desktop, tablet, phone in landscape and the Xbox browser, installable).

### A richer look
- **Sea gusts**: darker cat's-paw patches run downwind over the sea with calm slicks between (High and Ultra, off with the Waves comfort switch).
- **Ground detail**: grass clumps, tufts and dry patches, and wind ripples on sand, drawn by the ground shader with no new textures (High and Ultra; Settings › Ground detail). Cliffs keep their strata.
- **Richer houses**: stone footings, overhanging roofs, ridge beams, taller capped chimneys and door steps in ten town styles, near the ship on High and Ultra; far away and on Low the simple house stays.
- **Bloom on Ultra**: lit windows, lanterns and the moon glow softly at night; nothing at day (Settings › Glow).

### Lighter on memory
- **Ultra on a 4K TV** now holds 321 MB before bloom (330 MB with it), under a 355 MB budget, so it fits the Xbox browser better.
