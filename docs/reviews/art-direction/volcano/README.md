# Volcano smoke and eruptions: art notes, round 1

Shots looked at: /mnt/project-files/volcano/review-1/ (1–5, plus eruption by day in portrait). The mechanism is right; the look is not ready for Guillaume yet.

## 1. Puffs read as rocks
They look like rocks because the faces are shaded hard, they are the same grey as the cone, and they are biggest right at the crater.
- **Cut the facet contrast.** Give the smoke material a strong self-lit part (emissive about 0.55–0.65 of its colour) so the faces barely differ in brightness. This is the main fix.
- **Colours.** Quiet steam is a warm off-white, about `#ece8e1`, lighter than anything on the island. Eruption ash is a warm dark grey-brown, about `#5f5953`: darker than the cone, never blue.
- **Size over a puff's life.** Start small at the vent, grow as it rises and drifts, then shrink away only at the very end. Vary the size by about ±35% and squash it a little (y about 0.8).
- **Shape.** One leaning column downwind, tight at the vent and widening along the wind, not a heap on the rim.

## 2. The Smoulder's height (crater about 79, camera about 93)
- Rise only about 15–25 units above the rim, then travel mostly sideways with the wind.
- Camera guard: a puff whose top comes within about 12 units of the camera height shrinks out early. In shot 4 (sailing view), puffs fill a third of the screen. That must not happen.

## 3. Night glow
- In an eruption at night, mix the lower puffs toward lava orange, about `#ff7a3a`. Use roughly 40% for the first third of each puff's life, fading to plain ash above. Use the unlit glow batch or the instance colour (the shared lava-material route of art bible §6).
- A quiet plume at night gets only the night tint, with no glow.

## 4. Lava bombs read as orange confetti
They are spread over the whole island, the sea and the top of the screen.
- Use about 8–12 per burst.
- Keep the arcs short, landing on the cone's upper slopes within about 1.2 times the crater radius.
- Make them a bit bigger and fewer, not many and small. They are the punctuation, not the show.

## Small things
- Emberhold's vent trail drifts over the sea like a line of grey pebbles. Make it off-white steam, smaller, and about half as long.
- The ground fumaroles are fine. The night cracks look great.
