# v1.9 ring towns: after (issue #514)

Decision [0303](../../../decisions/0303-believable-ring-towns-streets-rows-round-the-harbour-lanes-o.md). The seven towns of the home ring now pass the town rules with no finding (`npm run island -- audit`). Lantern Rock and Teeth Rock have no houses. The before audit and plans are in the [README](README.md).

| Island | Before (fail / warn) | After | What changed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Abbey Head | 1 / 11 | 0 / 0 | a shore lane, the houses in two rows on a wider shelf, the side pier at the shore |
| Port Calda | 0 / 28 | 0 / 0 | three streets along the bay and one up from the pier, 32 cabins in rows, lamps along the streets |
| Saltmere | 5 / 29 | 0 / 0 | two streets and a waterfront lane, the fish-houses in rows round the bay, harbour office and warehouse on the west quay, the net green drawn together |
| Skerry | 3 / 4 | 0 / 0 | the path shortened, two paths to the plots, the stone house turned |
| Stonehaven | 13 / 21 | 0 / 0 | the cliff pulled back for a harbour quarter of three rows, seven houses by the keep, new stairs up the face; crane, blocks and quarry where they were |
| Watchman Key | 1 / 9 | 0 / 0 | one lane, the houses in a row along it |
| Westholm | 2 / 48 | 0 / 0 | three streets and a waterfront lane, the church and bakery by the harbour, the four north fields moved to the east and west blocks |

Signature places kept: Saltmere's smokehouse row, net green and East Reef buoys; Calda's slipway, cutter and pines; Westholm's mill on the hill; Stonehaven's crane, stacked blocks and idle quarry; Skerry's pier, plot stakes and signpost. No island, town level or quest landing moved (`npm run quest -- check` passes).
