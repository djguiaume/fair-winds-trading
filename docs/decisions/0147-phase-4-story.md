# 0147. The Phase 4 story: letters, keepers and outposts

**Status:** Accepted, 2026-10-24. Written by the head storyteller, who owns story decisions ([0129](0129-storyteller-brief.md)), after Guillaume asked that each biome get its own arc and that quests explain why a main port crafts only from raw goods it receives (far goods thread, 2026-10-24).

## Context

[0137](0137-phase-4-biomes-and-founded-outposts.md) gave Phase 4 four biomes, each with an inhabited main port and an outpost the player founds, and said the storyteller signs off new people. [0146](0146-far-goods-from-the-biome-outposts.md) set the far goods: the outpost gathers, the main port's keeper crafts, the ring buys, and the seas open south, west, north, east. It left the keepers, the openers' quests and the story link to the storyteller brief.

## Decision

The [storyteller brief](../story/storyteller-brief.md#phase-4-the-four-seas) gains a binding **Phase 4 section**:

1. **Every sea is three arcs:** a one-step letter (or chart) from a ring person whose relative went beyond the reef, the keeper's 3-step arc at the main port, and founding the outpost with that relative, which ends with the flagpole and name board ([0134](0134-fair-winds-epilogue.md)).
2. **The seas open one by one:** the south once `fair-winds` is done, each next letter once the previous outpost is founded.
3. **Names:** main ports Sunreach, Emberhold, Rimewick and Fogwatch; keepers Juno Reyes, Bram Kettle, Sigrun Vale and Elias Morrow; relatives Tam Ness, Oskar Calder, Nell Marlow and Pip Holt; outpost islands Palm Key, Cinder Isle, Snowberry Isle and Amber Strand. Each person's way of speaking is in the brief.
4. **Quest ids** follow `<port>-<slug>` and `outpost-<biome>` (table in the brief). The letters are new quests, so merged ring arcs never change.
5. **"No raw goods, no craft" is told in the story:** the keeper's arc shows an idle workshop with nobody to gather, and the relative's done card says where the raw goods go.

## Consequences

- Phase 4 arc threads can start from the brief without asking Guillaume. They ask the storyteller.
- A map or balance thread may rename a place only with the storyteller's OK.
- Rewards stay content for the balance pass, in the quest files.
- The planned Watchman Key arc becomes `watchman-passage`, the south's letter.
