# 0178. Volcano smoke and eruptions

**Status:** Proposed (2026-10-25). Waiting for the art director's look pass and Guillaume's OK on the screenshots ([docs/reviews/volcano](../reviews/volcano/README.md)). Split from the west fire sea ([0171](0171-the-west-fire-sea-emberhold.md)), which points here.

## Context

The west sea's volcano, **The Smoulder**, is "always smoking", and with a switch on it "erupts every few minutes while the boat is near"; Bram's arc ends with one story eruption that "plays whatever the switch says", a spectacle only, and "nothing in the copy needs the captain to have watched it" ([storyteller brief](../story/storyteller-brief.md#each-seas-hazards-and-set-pieces-guillaume-2026-10-24)). The art bible asks for "a thin smoke plume", glowing lava, and particles that stay cheap on the 2 GB tablet: one shared batch per kind, no new transparency ([art bible](../art/art-bible.md) rules 3 and 8). Every annoyance gets its own Settings switch (Guillaume), every number lives in a data file ([0093](0093-data-driven-content-and-authoring-tools.md)), and every quest effect goes through the quest engine.

## Decision

1. **Every crater smokes.** A volcano is a peak with a `crater` in an island file ([0171](0171-the-west-fire-sea-emberhold.md), `hasVolcano`); nothing else marks it. The island model reports each crater's centre, rim height, pool radius and flank slope (`IslandModel.craters`), read from the very geometry it builds, so smoke always sits on the crater the builder drew. A crater lets go low-poly puffs (one opaque, flat-shaded icosahedron each) that rise, drift with the wind more the higher they get, grow and then shrink away. No transparency, so no sorting or overdraw. A smaller crater gets a smaller, lower plume in proportion (`smoke.craterRadius`): Emberhold's and Cinder Isle's vents smoke a little, The Smoulder a lot. A crater coming in sight already has its whole plume.
2. **Eruptions near the boat.** With **Settings › Eruptions** on (default on, EN and FR), a big crater (`eruptions.minCrater`, so the small vents never do) erupts after `firstSec` once the boat comes within `nearUnits`, then every `everySec` [min, max] while she stays. An eruption builds over `rampSec`, holds `holdSec` and dies down over `fadeSec`: more, bigger, darker ash puffs, glowing lava bombs on arcs (one shared unlit batch) that come down on the cone's flank or in the sea with a puff of ash or steam, and the shared lava material (rivers, cracks, the crater pool) brightens by `lavaGain`. Off, volcanoes only smoke.
3. **A story eruption is quest data:** a reward kind `erupt: ["the-smoulder"]` (any island with a volcano; the quest checks say so otherwise). The quest engine sends an `erupt` event when it pays the reward in play (never when a quest test skips past it), and the view plays it **whatever the switch says**: at once if the volcano is in sight, else as soon as the boat comes within sight (`sightUnits`), dropped after `storyWaitSec`. It holds longer (`storyHoldSec`). Not saved: it is spectacle only and nothing needs it. `emberhold-glass` pays it when done (Bram's furnace relit, "Hear the Smoulder rumbling along?"). Cinder Isle's rising stays with 0166's rule (only while the boat is far or docked).
4. **Numbers and colours** are in `src/data/tuning/weather.json` › `volcano` (`sightUnits`; `smoke`: rate, life, rise, drift, sizes, spread, colour, full-size crater; `eruptions`: when and how long, smoke rate, size, rise and colour, bombs per second, speeds, gravity, sizes and colour, lava gain), each with its line in `tuning-meta.ts`, so all of it is in `?tune`.
5. **Editors and tools.** Craters are made in the island builder and `npm run island -- mountain … --crater` (0171). The quest builder's reward form has an **Eruption** field listing the map's volcanoes; `npm run quest -- check` checks it. `npm run shots -- --erupt the-smoulder --wait 9000` shoots one; `--no-eruptions` turns the switch off.
6. **Cost (measured headless, Phase 4 sea at The Smoulder, Low quality):** two extra draw calls in all (puffs, bombs), about 25 puffs quiet and up to ~100 puffs and ~50 bombs mid-eruption (about 2k extra triangles at most), 0.06 to 0.1 ms CPU per frame for the update, nothing at all when no crater is within `sightUnits`. Nothing in the sim changes: route, balance and level checks are untouched (balance run on all three levels passes).

## Consequences

- A new volcano anywhere is an island file with a crater; it smokes and, if big enough, erupts with no code. A new story eruption is one reward line.
- The lava material is shared by every island, so an eruption brightens lava everywhere in view for its length; only the volcano in sight erupts, so in practice this is the volcano's own lava.
- Not built: the big-event panel idea ([0174](0174-event-panel-for-big-story-events.md)) that could frame the story eruption, ash fall on the deck, sound. Each can be added on top later.
