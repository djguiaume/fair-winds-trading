# 0194. Open the edge seas after each ring arc

**Status:** Accepted (2026-10-25, Guillaume picked "Yes, open them earlier"; ruling by the head storyteller).

## Context

The edge seas are the biggest new part of Phase 4, but their first letter (`watchman-passage`)
waited for the ring's epilogue (`fair-winds`), and each sea waited for the previous sea's outpost
([0147](0147-phase-4-story.md)). That put the first far sea about 3 to 5 hours in and the last about
5 to 7 hours in (estimates from leg times, [Phase 4 review](../reviews/phase-4-review/README.md)).
Guillaume asked whether they could open earlier to keep players interested. Sailing out was never
blocked: the reef gateways are open, sailing in lifts the fog and the far main ports trade once
sighted.

## Decision

1. `watchman-passage` (Ness, Watchman Key) starts once `stonehaven-light` is done and the captain
   docks at Watchman Key. Ness's card already opens with "That lens put me in mind…". The south stays
   the first door: Ness's chart is how the captain learns to cross the reef.
2. Each other letter starts once its writer's own ring arc **and** `watchman-passage` are done:
   `calda-letter` after `calda-slipway`, `saltmere-letter` after `saltmere-shoals`,
   `westholm-letter` after `westholm-mill` (built by the east quests thread).
3. Keeper arcs and outposts keep their chains (letter, keeper, outpost). `fair-winds` stays after the
   four ring arcs; `four-winds` still waits for all four outposts. The seas run side by side.
4. No far port is locked: ports trade once sighted, the keeper only offers an arc after the letter,
   and a letter whose port was already found completes on the next docking. Outposts stay sites until
   their arc. No card text changes; the reason letters come now is that word has spread of a captain
   sailing beyond the reef.

## Consequences

- The first far sea opens about an hour in (after Ada's arc), all four once the ring arcs are done,
  about 3 to 4 hours in (estimates until the balance thread's story-order bots measure them).
- Crafted far goods can arrive before any ring town is a Port town, the only buyers today: the
  balance thread decides how they sell early.
- The storyteller updates the brief's start table and "the seas open one by one".
