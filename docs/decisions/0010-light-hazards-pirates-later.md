# 0010. Light hazards now, pirates and enemy factions later

**Status:** Accepted, 2026-10-19 (refines [0002](0002-trade-over-combat.md))

**Context:** Choice between no danger and light hazards.

**Decision:** Early phases have light hazards only (storms, reefs and shallows that slow the ship or spoil cargo). Pirates come in a later phase, and enemy factions possibly after that.

**Consequences:** Ship health/cargo damage systems should be designed so combat can be added later without reworking them. Trade and sailing stay the core.
