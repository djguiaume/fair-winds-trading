# 0009. Sandbox game for short play sessions

**Status:** Accepted, 2026-10-19

**Context:** Choice between an open sandbox and goal-based scenarios.

**Decision:** Open-ended sandbox, designed for short sessions to pass time: quick to load, quick to resume, progress auto-saved.

**Consequences:** No campaign or scripted scenarios for now. Contracts and settlement growth provide short-term goals inside the sandbox.
