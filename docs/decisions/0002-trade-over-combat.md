# 0002. Focus on sailing and trade, not combat

**Status:** Accepted, 2026-10-19

**Context:** The visual inspiration (*Privateers: Pirate Wars*) is a naval combat game. Guillaume liked its style but wants the game oriented toward sailing boats and transporting goods, later adding automated transport lines that grow settlements and push demand toward farther, harder-to-reach places.

**Decision:** The core loop is wind-based navigation and delivering goods. Ship-to-ship combat is out of scope; any hazards (storms, pirates to outrun) are optional and light.

**Consequences:** Difficulty comes from wind, distance, currents and route design rather than enemies. See [roadmap](../roadmap.md).
