# 0127. The tutorial becomes the prologue: "The foghorn"

**Status:** Implemented for review (2026-10-23). Guillaume's idea, written up in the [community arcs](../story/community-arcs.md#the-tutorial-and-prologue-the-foghorn) story he approved ([0122](0122-community-arcs-story.md)). It builds on the quest engine ([0125](0125-quest-engine.md)), goes with the home island ([0126](0126-home-island-build-spots.md)) and changes the lessons of [0025](0025-optional-first-voyage-tutorial.md), [0061](0061-menus-and-coach-marks.md) and [0113](0113-levels-tutorial-and-music-as-data.md) on purpose.

## Context

Today the tutorial starts near Saltmere: open the chart, pick any port, sails and wind, dock, buy, find a buyer, deliver, sell. The story turns it into the prologue. You start alone at sea and learn the sails and the wind. A foghorn sounds and the compass marks where it comes from. You find a small boat stuck on a reef and come alongside slowly without touching the reef. Harbourmaster Rook climbs aboard and asks to go home, so you pick Saltmere on the chart and dock. He thanks you with an island of your own, and then suggests a first cargo: buy, deliver and sell, as today. A player who skips the tutorial, or whose voyage finished it before this change, gets Rook's thanks and the island the next time they dock at Saltmere.

## Decision

1. **Same file, same runner, new order.** `first-voyage.json` keeps its format and its runner (`tutorial.ts`). The lessons are now: `sails`, `wind`, `foghorn`, `wreck`, `chart` (Rook asks for home), `home-port` (picking Saltmere, if the chart was opened first), `home` (sail there and dock), `island`, then the unchanged `buy`, `choose-delivery`, `deliver` and `sell`. The old `choose-port` and `dock` stay at the end, only as the way back when a lesson loses its harbour (sailing off before buying, cargo gone).
2. **The map holds the prologue's places** in an optional `prologue` block: `start` (where a voyage that takes the tutorial begins, a stretch of open sea) and `wreck` (the terrain feature whose wreck landmark is Rook's boat). On the Windward Ring that is East Reef, whose wreck was already drawn; the wreck's spot is now worked out in the sim (`src/sim/landmarks.ts`) and drawn from the same numbers. A map without a `prologue` (the first test sea, editor maps) skips the two sea lessons and starts at the chart.
3. **Two new events for marks at sea**, in the step's `mark` (`within`, and for coming alongside `maxKn` and `holdSec`): `reached-mark` (within range of the wreck) and `alongside-mark` (within range, slower than `maxKn`, for `holdSec` seconds). Touching a reef or a shore (new hull damage) starts the hold again. Like every tutorial event, they only watch what the player does.
4. **The harbour is home.** A step with `origin: "home"` sets the lesson's harbour to the map's home port. On the chart, only that port counts as the pick and the coach mark points at it. Rook rides as a passenger from the wreck to Saltmere, taking no hold space.
5. **Starting at sea.** Choosing Start tutorial on a fresh voyage that hasn't moved puts the boat at `prologue.start`. Replaying the tutorial later from the menu starts where you are, with the compass pointing to the wreck.
6. **The island is an engine quest, `rooks-thanks`.** Its start is the tutorial reaching `island`, or the tutorial being done (finished or skipped). Its one step is docking at home. On the tutorial path it ends on the Saltmere dock that opens the `island` lesson. On the skip path and for old voyages it ends at the next Saltmere dock, and Rook's card ([0124](0124-characters-look.md)) gives his thanks there. On the tutorial path the `island` lesson speaks for him instead, so no card covers it. **The island is this quest's reward**: `rooks-thanks` pays the home island's `homeIsland` reward ([0126](0126-home-island-build-spots.md)): Skerry becomes the captain's. No new save field. A new condition, `quest-at` (`quest`, `step`), holds once a quest has reached that step or finished; for the tutorial it asks the tutorial runner.
7. **On screen:** a brass mark on the wind compass shows the direction of the wreck during `foghorn` and `wreck`. A foghorn (`effects.foghorn` in `audio.json`, synthesised) sounds every few seconds during `foghorn`. A small waving figure stands on the wreck until Rook is aboard.
8. **Proof.** The random-session hash in `tests/plays-the-same.test.ts` was taken to prove 0113 changed nothing. It is retaken here on purpose, because the lessons change, and the random player now also sails near the wreck so its sessions reach every lesson. The trading lessons are still checked step by step in `tests/tutorial.test.ts`, and the prologue and Rook's thanks in `tests/prologue.test.ts`.

## Consequences

- New voyages begin with the rescue and end the tutorial with a home island to build on (the home island work).
- A saved tutorial step from before the change still resumes: every old step id still exists. The first old lesson (`chart`) now comes after the sea lessons.
- Skipping the tutorial still gives the island, so nobody misses it.
- The lesson text for `foghorn`, `wreck`, `home-port`, `home` and `island`, and the new copy for `chart`, are in `en.json` and `fr.json`. Rook's portrait and the dialogue card come from the characters' look work.
