## 1. The line that replaces the tutorial offer (#532)

Shown as a small card when a new voyage begins, then it fades. It is the harbour talking, not Rook (he has not appeared yet), and it never says "tutorial".

- EN: "Your boat is ready, captain. Raise the sails when you like; the hints will show the way, and you can hide them any time."
- FR: « Votre bateau est prêt, capitaine. Hissez les voiles quand vous voulez ; les conseils vous guideront, et vous pouvez les masquer à tout moment. »

The lessons stay in the menu ("Replay tutorial", the sailing lessons). Nothing else changes in their words.

## 4. Opening place and hour (#533)

No story constraint beyond this:

- **Hour:** morning, soft light, fair weather, no storm. The rescue is Rook's morning call for help; "thank the tides" reads best in daylight.
- **Place:** open water a short sail from the East Reef, so the reef and Saltmere's headland are the land ahead and the foghorn is believable. Wind on the beam or from behind for the first step.
- **Keep out of sight:** Skerry. Rook names it later; the captain must not see it first.
- The wreck stays where it is (prologue wreck, East Reef). Nothing else in the view needs a story reason.
