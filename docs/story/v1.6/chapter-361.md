# v1.6 #361: the chapter after Four Winds (storyteller lines, EN + FR)

Two quests, 5 steps, 2 done cards. About 12 cards in total with the turn steps already written; the chapter stays short on purpose. Hazards off changes nothing (same spots, calm). Nothing can fail.

## The answer to the mark

The star over a wave is **Rook's father's mark**: "a light on the water, the sign for home". When the family left Skerry the winter Rook was small, his father sealed the island and sent a bottle, a plank, a tin and a compass out into the four seas so the sea would keep them for a captain who would come. Nobody died, nobody is lost: it is a family secret Rook had forgotten. The currents of the four seas drew every drift line to the quiet in the middle of the Stormbreak, where a chest is chained under a buoy bearing the mark. In it: the old Skerry flag and a letter on the lid in his hand. The flag goes up on Skerry's pier beside the company flag. No new sea, no new character, no peril, no pirates; Rook gets his moment (brisk, with a catch). The father is never shown and never said dead: "he sent these out to wait".

Where: the spot is the quiet in the middle of the Stormbreak belt (the same spot as Elias's lull: use the lull spot of the east turn step so the player already knows it). The quest places a strange buoy there. The chest is a quest object.

## What Skerry gains (for the art director, `after:homecoming`)

A second mast on the pier with the old flag (the star over a wave, faded) beside the company flag, a long table on the pier with bunting and lanterns, a lantern at the pier end as a sea mark. No new buildings, no new boats. Everything small, within the island budget.
